A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Turning curiosity into useful work.

I enjoy working across the data journey—from collecting and cleaning information to analysing it, visualising patterns, and communicating what the results mean.

I’m studying for a Bachelor of Science in Data Science at The Co-operative University of Kenya. My interests include data analytics, geospatial analysis, healthcare information systems, and building accessible tools that help people work with data.

My experience includes an industrial attachment with the Social Health Authority in Makueni County, where I was exposed to record handling, data cleaning, front-desk operations, field activities, and healthcare administration workflows.

I value learning by doing, sharing knowledge, and improving through feedback. I’m continuing to strengthen my technical skills through projects and community learning.
